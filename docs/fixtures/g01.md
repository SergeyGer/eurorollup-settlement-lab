# Fixture G01 — one investor and two investment lifecycles

G01 is the portfolio golden fixture: one synthetic investor, a funded inventory
account, a separate fund vault and a fixture clock. It proves that cash becomes
an asset at a precisely stated moment and that the same numbers appear in the
UI, the API and the CLI.

## Inputs

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Opening cash | 1,000.00 dEUR (6 decimals, `1000000000` base units) |
| Spot instrument | tWETH, 18 decimals, fixture mark 2,000.00 EUR |
| Spot quote | EUR 200.00 at 2,000.00 EUR per tWETH, fixed investor charge EUR 0.05 |
| Fund instrument | eFUND, 6 decimals, fictional euro short-duration bond fund |
| Fund request | EUR 300.00, indicative NAV 100.00 EUR, baseline cutoff Friday 16:00 UTC |
| Authorized NAV | 96.00 EUR per unit for the assigned dealing date |
| Redemption case | one unit redeemed at NAV 98.00 EUR |

The fixture clock is a separate signed source: quote expiry, price staleness and
NAV observation times are read from the fixture, never from wall-clock time.

## Expected checkpoints

| Investor checkpoint | Spendable dEUR | Reserved dEUR | tWETH | eFUND |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seed | 1,000.00 | 0.00 | 0 | 0 |
| Buy EUR 200 at EUR 2,000 per tWETH | 799.95 | 0.00 | 0.100000 | 0 |
| Request EUR 300 at indicative NAV EUR 100 | 499.95 | 300.00 | 0.100000 | 0 |
| Strike and settle at NAV EUR 96 | 499.95 | 0.00 | 0.100000 | 3.125000 |

Closing portfolio value at the fixture marks of EUR 2,000 and EUR 96 is EUR
999.95. The EUR 300 pending cash is displayed once; on allocation it is replaced
by 3.125000 units.

In the separate redemption case, one unit at EUR 98 brings spendable cash to
EUR 597.95 and units to 2.125000.

## Assertions

- Spendable cash after the spot fill is `799950000` base units (799.95 dEUR).
- The investor charge is `50000` base units (0.05 dEUR), recorded as its own leg.
- Issued units are `3125000` base units (3.125000 eFUND), exactly the floor of
  300.00 / 96.00.
- Reserved cash returns to zero only on allocation; it briefly equals 300.00
  dEUR while the fund request is awaiting NAV.
- The business journal balances to zero per asset across investor, dealer,
  escrow, issuer treasury, fee recipient and issuance control accounts.
- Re-running the fixture from its stored events produces the same business
  digest.

## Boundary and exceptional cases

The G01 family also covers:

- a spot quote older than 60 seconds (refused before acceptance);
- a reverse quote with insufficient inventory and a minimum-proceeds check;
- a fund request with a zero-unit outcome (must be rejected, not allocated);
- a dust residual that returns to the investor instead of being retained;
- a cancellation before the cutoff (reservation released) and after the cutoff
  (not cancellable by the investor);
- a missing or stale NAV (cash stays reserved, no units are minted).
