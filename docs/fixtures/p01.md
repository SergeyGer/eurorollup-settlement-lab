# Fixture P01 — the Friday cutoff experiment

P01 is separate from the single-investor portfolio example G01. It contains two
independent EUR 300 fund subscriptions from two verified investors, each
starting with EUR 1,000, and drives the deterministic simulation engine used by
the AI Policy Lab.

## Frozen inputs

- Dealing days: Friday 20 November 2026 and Monday 23 November 2026 in calendar
  `LAB-UTC-1`. Saturday and Sunday are closed.
- NAVs arrive at 17:00 UTC: Friday at 96.00 EUR, Monday at 100.00 EUR. Separate
  signed observations exist for each date.
- Orders accepted strictly before the cutoff receive that day's NAV. An order
  exactly at the cutoff advances to the next dealing day.
- Baseline rule: eFUND subscription cutoff Friday 16:00 UTC.
- Candidate rule: eFUND subscription cutoff Friday 15:00 UTC.

| Order received Friday | Baseline, Friday 16:00 | Candidate, Friday 15:00 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F-1450, 14:50 UTC | Friday allocation: 300.00 / 96.00 = 3.125000 units | Friday allocation: 3.125000 units; arrived before both cutoffs |
| F-1530, 15:30 UTC | Friday allocation: 300.00 / 96.00 = 3.125000 units | Monday allocation: 300.00 / 100.00 = 3.000000 units; missed the proposed cutoff |

## Expected portfolio and timing differences

| Snapshot across both accounts | Baseline | Candidate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friday 17:01 UTC | 1,400.00 spendable, 0.00 reserved, 6.250000 units | 1,400.00 spendable, 300.00 reserved, 3.125000 units |
| Monday 17:01 UTC | 1,400.00 spendable, 0.00 reserved, 6.250000 units | 1,400.00 spendable, 0.00 reserved, 6.125000 units |

The result card reads "1 of 2 orders changes dealing date" and "EUR 300 more
reserved at Friday close". F-1530 settles 72 hours later and receives 0.125000
fewer units at the supplied Monday NAV. The reduction is a property of these
fixture prices, not a fee. Both branches spend EUR 600; the difference in unit
count is distinct from cash spent.

## Boundary fixtures

Separate cases at 14:59:59, 15:00:00 and 16:00:00 UTC on Friday have expected
baseline/candidate dealing days of Fri/Fri, Fri/Mon and Mon/Mon.

Calendar variants:

- Monday marked closed, with a Tuesday NAV: the candidate F-1530 assignment must
  move to Tuesday.
- Monday open but its NAV missing: the candidate F-1530 stays pending with EUR
  300 reserved and units outstanding; the Friday snapshot is unchanged from the
  normal candidate snapshot.

## Assertions

- The confirmed patch is the only variable between the two branches; intents,
  observations, opening snapshot, calendar and fixture seed are identical.
- Both branches produce identical canonical business hashes in the browser, the
  local service and the CLI.
- The run is idempotent: a same-key same-body retry returns the existing run, a
  changed body under a reused key returns `409`.
- A failed run publishes no partial result.
