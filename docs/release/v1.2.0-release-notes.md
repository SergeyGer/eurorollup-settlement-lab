# v1.2.0 — Settlement and protocol infrastructure

`v1.2.0` delivers the post-1.0 infrastructure milestone from the release audit brief:
settlement data availability, on-chain eligibility, sponsored orders, a machine-verifiable
settlement pack, a consensus-backed lane with blob DA and an independent deriving verifier,
canonical reconciliation and reorg recovery, operations benchmarks and money/authority
reconciliation, and independent acceptance.

## What is included

- **SET-01–SET-04** — deterministic settlement slice, on-chain eligibility with real
  UltraHonk proofs and rotation, sponsored orders with negative paths, and a settlement pack
  whose canonical witness and tamper refusal are machine-verifiable.
- **NET-01** — consensus-backed lane with pinned clients, blob DA transaction and beacon
  sidecar, L1 finality, and an independent deriving verifier matching the sequencer block
  hash; the earlier image/genesis blocker and its resolution stay on record.
- **LED-01** — exact-block-hash anchors, noncanonical-anchor rejection, orphan marking,
  rewind/replay, release-once reservations, duplicate delivery, interrupted restart and
  per-asset conservation.
- **OPS-01/OPS-02** — six-run batch-setting benchmark (4 and 12 L1 blocks; nearest-rank
  p50/p95, L1 expenditure, per-success cost, selected 4 blocks), money/authority
  reconciliation, RPC failover, batcher pause/resume, indexer restore and a timelocked
  two-of-three upgrade rehearsal with forward recovery.
- **Independent acceptance** — 19 gates executed from a fresh clone of the accepted commit
  and the clean accepted worktree, with explicit attribution of what was executed versus
  verified from evidence.
